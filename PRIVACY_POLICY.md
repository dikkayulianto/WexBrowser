# Privacy Policy for VexBrowser

**Last updated:** September 15, 2026

VexBrowser ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use
**VexBrowser does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).**
- **Browsing History & Bookmarks:** All browsing history, cookies, and saved bookmarks are stored locally on your device only. They are never sent to our servers or any third-party servers.
- **Incognito Mode:** When using Incognito Mode, browsing data, cookies, and temporary cache are discarded immediately upon closing the tab.
- **No Analytics / Telemetry:** VexBrowser does not use third-party tracking libraries, advertising SDKs, or analytics trackers.

### 2. Permissions Used
VexBrowser requests the following permissions strictly for core browser functionalities:
- **`INTERNET`**: Required to load websites and web content upon user request.
- **`ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`**: Required to detect network availability (Wi-Fi or Cellular) to handle offline states properly.
- **`WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` (API <= 28)**: Required solely if the user chooses to download a file from the internet to their device.

### 3. Built-in Content Blocking (VexShield)
VexBrowser includes a local on-device content shield (**VexShield**) that intercepts unwanted advertisements, pop-ups, and trackers within the browser's own web view.
- Blocked count statistics (e.g. number of ads blocked, estimated data saved) are calculated and stored **strictly on your device** via local storage.
- No URLs or browsing activities are reported back to us.

### 4. Third-Party Websites
When you browse the web using VexBrowser, you may visit third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any website you visit.

### 5. Children’s Privacy
VexBrowser does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected from any user, children's personal data is not collected.

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

### 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via Google Play Console developer contact email.
